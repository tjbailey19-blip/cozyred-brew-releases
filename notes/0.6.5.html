Brew 0.6.5

Brew turns the raw footage of a recorded conversation into shorts, cut most of the way, and puts them in your Premiere timeline for you to finish. In Brew's panel inside Premiere you choose the episode folder; Brew lines up the cameras and mics, listens to them, finds the conversations and makes ready shorts. Click one to put it in your timeline, or pick a beat and press Make the cut.

What Brew needs

A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later, Adobe Premiere Pro 25.6 or later, and 10 GB free. An internet connection the first time it opens (it downloads the transcription model once) and whenever it reads an episode with Claude.

Install

  1. Download Brew-0.6.5.zip below and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag Brew into your Applications folder and open it from there. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet. Brew has no window: a cup appears in the menu bar at the top right of your screen, and Brew's first screen opens in your browser.
  3. On that screen, paste the Claude key and the invite code you were sent, then press Keep.
  4. Open Premiere and a project, then Window > UXP Plugins > Brew. Press Choose your episode folder.

The Claude key and the invite code

Paste the Claude key and the invite code you were sent. Both came with your invite; paste each one whole. Brew keeps the key in your Mac's Keychain and uses it only for your own requests. If you have a Claude key of your own, it comes from console.anthropic.com (it starts with sk-ant-).

What leaves your Mac

Your footage, audio and frames never leave your Mac: only the words of the transcript go to Anthropic, under your own key, and the whole list of what Brew sends and to whom is TRUST.md inside Brew.app, beside NOTICES.txt (in Applications, right-click Brew, Show Package Contents, then Contents, Resources).

Uninstall

Click the cup in the menu bar and choose Uninstall Brew…: Brew moves itself to the Trash, takes its panel out of Premiere and forgets your Claude key. Uninstall and Remove Projects deletes its data folder too.
